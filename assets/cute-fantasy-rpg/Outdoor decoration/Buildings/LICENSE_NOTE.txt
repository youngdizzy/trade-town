The building sprites in this folder (Fisherman_House, Blacksmith_House,
Inn, Church, Shed, GreenHouse, Windmill, Barn, and House_5_Limestone) are
sourced from the Cute Fantasy PREMIUM pack (kenmi-art.itch.io/cute-fantasy-rpg),
not the free version used by every other file under cute-fantasy-rpg/.
Church_Red_Front.png and GreenHouse_Wood_Front.png are single frames
cropped from that pack's multi-frame building sheets.
